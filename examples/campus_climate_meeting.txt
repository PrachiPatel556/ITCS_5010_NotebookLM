Campus Sustainability Committee minutes, March 2026

The committee approved a provisional capital budget of 1.2 million dollars for the library and science hall heat-pump conversion. It reserved 400,000 dollars for the electric shuttle purchase and chargers, and 150,000 dollars for the laboratory freezer replacement program. These allocations must be confirmed after the October 2026 energy audit. The committee asked procurement to prioritize equipment that can be maintained by local technicians and to report any expected schedule changes at the next monthly meeting.

Student representatives asked how the public could verify the emissions target. Facilities said the LEAF-7 dashboard would publish energy and emissions updates once per quarter, starting with a 2022 baseline table. Each update would identify which figures were directly metered and which were estimates. The committee wanted the dashboard to explain any revisions rather than silently replacing old numbers. It also requested an accessible summary of the audit after completion.

The committee discussed purchasing carbon offsets as a shortcut. It rejected that proposal because offsets would not reduce energy consumed by campus buildings or vehicles. Members instead supported the heat-pump, shuttle, and freezer measures in the plan. The chair noted that the six diesel shuttles must be replaced by June 2027 to keep the fleet project on schedule. No dean or university president was named in these minutes.
